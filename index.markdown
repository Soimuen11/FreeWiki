## About this wiki

The Free Wiki started on June 4th 2020 as a collection of the issues I,
Philippe Iannetta, faced on my Linux machine and in my work as a sysadmin.
It has since grown into something broader: a reference that any sysadmin,
professional or home user, can pick up and use.

The Sysadmin section does not describe one particular infrastructure. It
collects good practices that apply anywhere Linux or PowerShell is used.
Networking may be added later.

It remains incomplete, and I suspect it always will be. This is why I believe
everyone should make this wiki theirs. If you wish to see something in it, you
need only ask. If you have suggestions, do not hesitate to contact me or send
me a pull request.

In August 2026, Luca from [semplicemente.io](https://semplicemente.io) gave a
more modern look to the FreeWiki. I would like to officially thank him here as
I find this new design much better than the previous one. Dont' hesitate to
check out [his work](https://semplicemente.io)!
